# Video and drawing tools

- `brand-kit/` is the only source for Inlay's device pictures (see its README). `kitgen.js`, `kit2android.py` and `geo.js` turn it into the app's `assets/devices`.
- `kb2.js` and `spotlight4.js` draw the keyboard for the brand kit's approved mock-ups (`brand-kit/in-use.html`).
- `ctrl_paths.json`, `ds4-layout-points.json` and `trace-controllers.py` are the traces the controller drawings came from (the DualShock 4 from the CC BY 3.0 Wikimedia drawing).
- The Roboto fonts (Apache 2.0) are Android's own, used wherever a screen is shown.

The README images and videos are made in `docs/visuals-src` (see its README).
