# Device pictures

The controller and handheld drawings in `app/src/main/assets/devices` come from Inlay's own brand kit
(`tools/video/brand-kit`), converted for the app by `kit2android.py`.

- The PlayStation-style controller outline is based on the DualShock 4 drawing by Tokyoship,
  licensed under CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
- The pictures are drawn in the app with AndroidSVG 1.4 by Paul LeBeau, licensed under the
  Apache License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
